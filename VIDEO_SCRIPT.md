# Pioneer Academy - Hackathon Presentation Script (3 Minutes)

**Target Audience:** Pi Core Team & Pioneers
**Goal:** Showcase utility, Pi integration, and user flow.

---

### 0:00 - 0:30 | Introduction & Problem

**(Visual: Title Slide "Pioneer Academy" -> Montage of people looking confused at crypto charts)**
**Speaker:** "Hello Pi Community! We are the team behind **Pioneer Academy**. We noticed a problem: The Web3 world is complex, and many Pioneers don't know how to use their Pi safely or avoid scams."

### 0:30 - 1:00 | The Solution (What is it?)

**(Visual: Screen recording of the App's Dashboard, scrolling through courses)**
**Speaker:** "Pioneer Academy is a Learn-to-Earn educational platform built directly for the Pi Ecosystem. We turn learning into a game. Pioneers take interactive courses on Wallet Security, KYC, and Blockchain basics."

### 1:00 - 1:45 | Pi Integration (The "How")

**(Visual: Clicking "Connect Wallet" -> Pi SDK request -> Guest Mode fallback)**
**Speaker:** "We've deeply integrated the **Pi SDK**.

1.  **Authentication:** Users sign in seamlessly with their Pi account.
2.  **Payments:** Note our 'Premium Store'. Users can spend Test-Pi to unlock exclusive avatars and XP boosts using the official Pi Payment flow."

### 1:45 - 2:30 | Walkthrough (User Flow)

**(Visual: User finishing a quiz, earning XP, seeing the confetti animation)**
**Speaker:** "Watch as a user completes the 'Avoiding Scams' module. They answer questions, learn instantly from explanations, and earn XP. This progress is saved using our dual Cloud-Local architecture, ensuring they never lose their hard work, even with spotty internet."

### 2:30 - 3:00 | Conclusion

**(Visual: Leaderboard screen -> "Thank You" slide with Team Name)**
**Speaker:** "We aren't just an app; we are building a safer, smarter generation of Pioneers. Pioneer Academy: Learn, Earn, and Grow with Pi. Thank you for watching!"
