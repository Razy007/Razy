# Privacy Policy for Pioneer Academy

**Effective Date:** 2024-12-17

## 1. Introduction

Pioneer Academy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application within the Pi Network ecosystem.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary for the functionality of the app:

- **Pi Network User ID (UID):** Used to identify your unique account and progress.
- **Username:** Displayed on your profile and leaderboard.
- **App Progress:** Course completion status, XP, Level, and virtual rewards.

**We do NOT collect:**

- **Passphrases:** We will NEVER ask for your Pi Wallet passphrase.
- **Personal Contact Info:** We do not collect emails, phone numbers, or physical addresses unless explicitly provided for support.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To maintain your educational progress and gamification stats.
- To verify unique users and prevent bot activity.
- To facilitate In-App Payments (if applicable) strictly via the Pi SDK.

## 4. Data Storage

- Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) and locally on your device for caching.
- We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

## 5. Third-Party Services

- **Pi Network:** We use the Pi SDK for authentication. Your interaction is subject to Pi Network's Privacy Policy.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your data from our servers. Contact us at support@pioneeracademy.app (placeholder) for data requests.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be notified within the app.
